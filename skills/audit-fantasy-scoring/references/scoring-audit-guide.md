# Scoring Audit Guide

Keep these layers separate:

1. Observed platform value.
2. Raw game statistic.
3. Configured base points.
4. Role-specific bonus.
5. Team win or stomp bonus.
6. Champion multiplier or bonus.
7. Per-game versus per-series aggregation.
8. Weekly total or average.

For coaches, evaluate the complete team's conditional win and loss scores.
For players, confirm that the role and scoring configuration match the target
round. Preserve screenshots as evidence, but do not assume cropped UI labels
contain the complete rule definition.
